1) What does *abstraction* in the sense of "removing detail" always cost
   you?
   #+begin_quote
   Removing detail always implies a loss of information. If you have
   less information, it is easier to fall for an illusion: each
   abstraction is the machine playing a trick that lets you get on
   with your work.
   #+end_quote

2) What are the three parts of a computer and what distinguishes them?
   #+begin_quote
   - The CPU is super-fast: access latency is about 0.3 ns (billionth)
   - The RAM is fast: access latency is about 100 ns
   - The NVM is super-slow: access latency of a hard disk is 10 ms

   If one CPU cycle was 1 second, then the RAM is a coffee break, a
   SSD is a full day, and a HDD is a year.
   #+end_quote

3) In Python, what is =-2 * 5**2 - 10=
   #+begin_src python :results output :exports both :session *Python* :python python3 
     print(-2 * 5**2 - 10) # -(2 * 25) - 10 = -60
   #+end_src

   #+RESULTS:
   : -60

4) In Python, what is =1/3= and why?
   #+begin_src python :results output :exports both :session *Python* :python python3 
     print(1/3) # appears as a float with 17 digits
     print((1/3).as_integer_ratio()[0].bit_length()) # stored on 53 bits
     print((3).as_integer_ratio()[0].bit_length()) # stored on 2 bits (2^0 + 2^1 = 11)
   #+end_src

   #+RESULTS:
   : 0.3333333333333333
   : 53
   : 2
