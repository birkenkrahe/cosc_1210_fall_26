* Tip Calculator

Write a Python program that computes the share of a bill given the
total bill amount, the tip percentage, and the size of the party.

Sample input: total bill = $45.50, tip = 15%, party size = 4
#+begin_example
45.50 15 4
#+end_example

Sample output:
#+begin_example
Your share: $13.08
#+end_example
